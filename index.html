About

Digital linguistics student at the University of Ljubljana, Slovenia — the closest thing I have to a label, but what I spend my time on has ended up somewhere between language, data, research and the internet.

This has to be my n-th attempt at making a personal site. I'm hoping this one sticks around for a while. As you probably noticed, this isn't meant to be a formal website; if you're looking for that, check out my CV (PDF) or visit my LinkedIn page.

Now

At the moment I am mainly occupied with the master's thesis, some language-related work and a few smaller side projects.

Lately I have spent a lot of time on Slovene on Bluesky, which recently turned into a paper on the Slovene Bluesky community, presented at JT-DH 2026 in Ljubljana, where it received the best student paper award.

The more I explore Bluesky and ATProto, the more interesting I find them, both as research objects and as actual alternatives for the social web. I'd very much like to see more people (and more Slovene!) there.

I've also been looking at the wider ATProto ecosystem, including W Social, which I wrote a (realistically critical) blog post about – as far as I'm concerned, the only detailed one in Slovene so far.

Language, data, etc.

Most of my academic and work experience has to do with corpora, annotation, language technology and datasets. I am mostly interested in how people actually use language.

Small internet things

I (try to) make websites and tools, mostly for myself, and usually spend more time on them than planned. I'm very much a vibecoder rather than a programmer, building these with help from generative AI coding tools and agents.

Beyond that

Over the years, I've also been involved in student representation and various youth organisations, mostly centred on practical questions around the quality of education, digitalisation, access to information and how institutions actually work.
